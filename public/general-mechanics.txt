SUNDAY FUNDAY DANCE COMPETITION 2026
OFFICIAL MECHANICS AND GUIDELINES

PRELIMINARY COMPETITION: December 6, 13, and 20, 2026
GRAND FINALS: December 27, 2026
VENUE: Ligao City Pavilion

I. OBJECTIVES
1. Provide children and youth with an opportunity to showcase their dancing talents, creativity, and artistic abilities.
2. Promote confidence, discipline, teamwork, sportsmanship, and camaraderie among participants.
3. Encourage children and youth to engage in productive, enjoyable, and creative activities.
4. Discover and recognize talented young performers in Hip-Hop, Dancesport, and TikTok Mashup Dance.
5. Promote family participation and community engagement through the city's Sunday Funday activities.

II. COMPETITION CATEGORIES AND ELIGIBILITY

GENERAL ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
• The Sunday Funday Dance Competition 2026 is open to bona fide residents of Ligao City, Albay, and nearby cities and municipalities.
• Participants from Ligao City and neighboring localities may join any of the three (3) competition categories, provided they meet the prescribed age, membership, and registration requirements.
• All participants must submit a valid identification card (ID) bearing their date of birth upon registration for verification by the Organizing Committee.
• Winners or champion groups from previous Sunday Funday Dance Competitions are NOT ELIGIBLE to participate in the 2026 competition. This restriction applies to all previous winning or champion groups, regardless of the competition category in which they previously participated.

A. HIP-HOP COMPETITION
• Open to residents of Ligao City and nearby cities and municipalities aged 7–22 years old, with each competing group consisting of 7 to 10 members, at least 3 of whom must be aged 7–12 years old, while the remaining members must be aged 13–22 years old.
• All members must submit the necessary registration documents and comply with the age and residency requirements.
• Each group must submit an audition video through the official submission link or QR Code provided by the organizing committee.
• Music, choreography, costumes, and dance movements must be appropriate for children and family audiences.
• Each performance shall have a minimum duration of three (3) minutes and a maximum duration of five (5) minutes.
• Only groups that successfully pass the audition and receive confirmation from the organizing committee shall be eligible to participate in the competition.

B. DANCESPORT COMPETITION
• Open to residents of Ligao City and nearby cities and municipalities aged 7–17 years old, with each competing entry consisting of a pair of two (2) partners.
• All members must submit the necessary registration documents and comply with the age and residency requirements.
• Each group must submit an audition video through the official submission link or QR Code provided by the organizing committee.
• Costumes, music, and choreography must be appropriate for children and youth.
• Each performance shall have a minimum duration of three (3) minutes and a maximum duration of five (5) minutes.
• Only groups that successfully pass the audition and receive confirmation from the organizing committee shall be eligible to participate in the competition.

C. TIKTOK MASHUP DANCE COMPETITION
• Open to residents of Ligao City and nearby cities and municipalities aged 7–22 years old, with each competing group consisting of 7 to 10 members, at least 3 of whom must be aged 7–12 years old, while the remaining members must be aged 13–22 years old.
• All members must submit the necessary registration documents and comply with the age and residency requirements.
• Each group must submit an audition video through the official submission link or QR Code provided by the organizing committee.
• Each group shall present a creative dance routine incorporating a mashup of popular TikTok-inspired dance trends and music.
• All music, lyrics, gestures, choreography, and costumes must be appropriate for children and family audiences.
• The performance shall demonstrate creativity, synchronization, rhythm, coordination, and overall entertainment value.
• Each performance in all three categories shall have a minimum duration of three (3) minutes and a maximum duration of five (5) minutes.
• Only groups that successfully pass the audition and receive confirmation from the organizing committee shall be eligible to participate in the competition.

III. PRELIMINARY COMPETITION MECHANICS
1. The preliminary competitions shall be conducted over three (3) consecutive Sundays: December 6, 13, and 20, 2026.
2. The competition shall feature three (3) categories: Hip-Hop, Dancesport, and TikTok Mashup Dance.
3. Each category shall accommodate 3 qualified entries or groups per preliminary competition date. Accordingly, 9 entries or groups shall compete each Sunday, with a total of 27 entries or groups across all three preliminary competition dates.
4. All participants shall meet and comply with the eligibility criteria, age requirements, and documentary and registration requirements stipulated in the Guidelines.
5. For all categories, each group must submit an audition video through the official submission link or QR Code provided by the organizing committee. No physical or in-person audition shall be required.
6. The submitted audition videos shall serve as the basis for the screening and evaluation of entries. Only groups that meet the prescribed requirements and receive confirmation from the organizing committee shall be allowed to participate in the preliminary competition.
7. The organizing committee shall verify the submitted videos, registration details, proof of age, and proof of residency before confirming the qualified participants and groups.
8. The organizing committee shall announce the list of qualified participants and groups, together with their assigned preliminary competition dates.
9. Each qualified participant, pair, or group shall perform according to the prescribed performance duration of three (3) to five (5) minutes.
10. All performances must comply with the official music requirements, choreography guidelines, costume standards, and safety regulations stated in these mechanics.
11. The Board of Judges shall evaluate each performance based on the official judging criteria provided under Section VII.
12. Winners shall be determined separately for each category on each preliminary competition date based on the scores awarded by the Board of Judges.

IV. GRAND FINALS MECHANICS
Schedule: December 27, 2026
1. The Grand Finals shall be conducted on the fourth Sunday of December 2026.
2. Qualified participants or groups from the preliminary competitions shall compete for the championship in their respective categories.
3. Each finalist shall deliver one final performance in accordance with the prescribed duration and competition rules.
4. Judges shall evaluate the final performances using the official judging criteria.
5. The participant, pair, or group with the highest total score in each category shall be declared Champion.
6. Two (2) consolation prizes shall be awarded in each category, subject to the final results and the number of eligible finalists.
7. Special awards shall be presented separately from the category championship and consolation prizes.
8. The Board of Judges' decisions shall be final, subject to verification of scores and correction of clerical errors.
9. The awarding ceremony shall be held after all performances and verification of results.

V. JUDGING CRITERIA
The following criteria shall be used for all three categories, with reasonable consideration for the characteristics of each dance style.

• Technique and Execution – 30%
• Rhythm, Timing, and Musicality – 25%
• Creativity and Choreography – 20%
• Stage Presence and Expression – 15%
• Costume and Overall Presentation – 10%
TOTAL: 100%

DESCRIPTION OF CRITERIA
1. Technique and Execution – Accuracy, coordination, control, and proper execution of dance movements.
2. Rhythm, Timing, and Musicality – Ability to perform movements in time with the music and maintain consistent rhythm.
3. Creativity and Choreography – Originality, variety, transitions, synchronization, and overall arrangement of the routine.
4. Stage Presence and Expression – Confidence, energy, audience engagement, and appropriate interpretation of the music.
5. Costume and Overall Presentation – Neatness, suitability, creativity, and appropriateness of costumes and overall presentation.

VIII. SPECIAL AWARDS
The following special awards shall be presented during the Grand Finals, with one (1) awardee to be selected across all competition categories for each special award:

A. CROWD'S FAVORITE AWARD
1. One (1) award shall be given to the finalist from any category who receives the highest audience support through the voting or scoring method announced by the organizing committee.
2. The voting procedure shall be clearly explained before the competition to ensure fairness and transparency.
3. The award carries a cash prize of ₱1,000.00.

B. BEST DRESSED PERFORMER
1. One (1) award shall be presented to the performer from any category who demonstrates outstanding costume creativity, neatness, originality, and suitability for a children's dance competition.
2. Costumes must be appropriate and safe for the participant's age and performance.
3. The award carries a cash prize of ₱1,000.00.

IX. SAFETY AND CHILD PROTECTION
1. The safety and welfare of all participants shall be a priority.
2. Parents or legal guardians shall be responsible for accompanying and supervising their children, subject to the event's child-protection arrangements.
3. The organizing committee shall implement appropriate crowd management and provide a safe performance area and reasonable access to first aid.
4. Photography and video recording shall comply with applicable consent and child-protection requirements.
5. Dangerous stunts, unsafe lifts, inappropriate gestures, and movements that may cause injury are prohibited.
6. No participant shall be required to perform movements beyond their capabilities or that compromise their safety.
7. All participants, coaches, guardians, and spectators must maintain respectful and orderly conduct.

XI. FINAL PROVISIONS
1. The organizing committee reserves the right to make reasonable adjustments to the program, schedule, and performance arrangements, when necessary, provided that participants are properly informed.
2. Matters not covered by these mechanics shall be resolved by the organizing committee in consultation with the Board of Judges.
3. All participants shall be deemed to have accepted these mechanics upon registration.
4. The organizing committee shall ensure that all rules, judging criteria, penalties, and award procedures are communicated before the competition begins.
5. These mechanics shall take effect upon approval by the authorized officials of the organizing office.
